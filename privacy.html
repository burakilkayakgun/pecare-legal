Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PeCare ("we", "us", "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application. We designed PeCare to keep most of your data on your device.

1. Summary

  • Pet and schedule data is stored locally on your phone unless a future version offers optional cloud sync.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Subscriptions are processed by Apple (and RevenueCat as our subscription management provider).
  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Pet profiles (name, species, breed, birthdate, optional photo)
  • Care schedules, reminders, and completion history
  • App preferences (e.g. default reminder time)

This data is stored in local app storage on your device.

Information collected automatically

  • Subscription status — Apple provides purchase receipts; we use RevenueCat to validate entitlements (e.g. PeCare Pro). RevenueCat may receive an anonymous app user ID and purchase events.
  • Crash or analytics data — If enabled in a future update, we may use a service such as Firebase Crashlytics or Sentry to improve stability. This policy will be updated before such collection begins.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account for the current version of PeCare.
  • We do not collect precise location for advertising.
  • We do not access your contacts or photos except when you explicitly pick a pet photo from your library.

3. How we use information

  • Provide reminders and care scheduling features
  • Validate and restore in-app subscriptions
  • Improve app reliability and fix bugs (if analytics/crash reporting is enabled)
  • Respond to support requests you send us

4. Third-party services

PeCare may interact with:

  • Apple App Store — in-app purchases and subscription billing
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement management (RevenueCat Privacy Policy)

These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

5. Data storage and retention

  • Local app data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • JSON export in Settings lets you keep a copy of your data.
  • Subscription records retained by Apple and RevenueCat are governed by their retention policies.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Notifications — control in iOS Settings and in the app.
  • Export — Settings → Export data (JSON).
  • Delete — uninstall the app to remove local data; cancel subscriptions in Apple ID settings.
  • Access / correction — you can edit pet and schedule data in the app.

Depending on where you live (e.g. EEA, UK, California), you may have additional rights to access, delete, or port personal data. Contact us at the email below.

7. Children

PeCare is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect data processed by our service providers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; you use the app at your own risk.

9. International users

If you use PeCare outside your home country, your information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate (including the United States).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app where appropriate.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: support@pecare.app (replace with your support email before publishing).
